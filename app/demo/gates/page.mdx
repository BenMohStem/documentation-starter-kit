# How It Is Tested

<span className="wwa-page-title">How it is tested</span>

A claim this project makes is worth exactly as much as the evidence
behind it, so testing is not a phase — it is the way anything comes to
exist here.

## The habit, in brief

- **Nothing ships on reputation.** A technique from a research paper, a
  hunch, an industry practice — all are treated the same way: build
  the smallest honest version, measure it on this machine, and let the
  number decide;
- **Failures are kept.** When an idea loses, the record of *how* it
  lost stays in the project forever — it is how the same mistake is
  never made twice;
- **The instruments are tested too.** The tools that measure the
  engine are audited against the engine itself before any number they
  produce is believed. Twice now, a promoted result turned out to be a
  measurement artifact — both times it was caught, retracted, and the
  testing made stronger;
- **Every release runs everything.** The full test battery and the
  playable demonstration must pass, or there is no release.

## What that buys

When the world finally runs — when the ecology lives and the economy
moves — its behavior will rest on parts that earned their place. That
is the whole reason the engine is built this way: **a world you can
trust is built from parts you can check.**

The full engineering record — every benchmark, incident, and retracted
claim — lives with the project's research archive and will be
published as the site grows.
