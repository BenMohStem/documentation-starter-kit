## What runs today

A from-scratch C engine — physics, a first-person 3D renderer, sound,
input, deterministic replay — and a playable demonstration built on
it: move through a small world with real bodies, real mass, real
contacts.

It is deliberately plain. This is the engine proving itself, not the
world selling itself: every frame you see is produced by rules that
were measured before they were trusted. Visual polish comes later, on
purpose — it is the cheapest thing to add and the easiest thing to
hide behind.

## What it proves

- Physics stepping live: bodies with mass, friction, contact;
- First-person movement and mouse-look through a real 3D scene;
- Deterministic replay: the same inputs produce the same world, every
  time;
- The engine survives its own tests on every release — the build
  refuses to ship if the demonstration cannot run.

## What it does not include yet

No animals, no ecology, no building, no economy — those systems are
being measured in isolation and enter the world one honest piece at a
time. This page will say exactly when that happens, and what each
piece proved.
