## What the demo includes

- First-person movement through a real 3D scene: WASD, mouse look, jump;
  a developer fly mode and pause;
- The physics core stepping live: bodies with mass, contacts, friction;
  warm-started persistent manifolds — the solver remembers between frames
  (measured 70.98× replay speedup at zero drift in the micro-benchmark);
- Broadphase switchable live between linked and packed cell layouts (**P**
  key) on a deterministic world — the packed path is the production
  default from the 2.10–2.13 spatial work;
- Generated PCM audio with rate-limited collision tones (**H**);
- Deterministic reset, headless bounded 120-frame validation runs with
  framebuffer proofs at start/mid/end.

## What it does not include (yet)

- No animals, plants, ecology — the mechanisms are measured prototypes
  ([Emergence](/world/emergence)) but are not in the shipped demo;
- No building, crafting, or machines — planned, not built;
- No save meta-world — planned;
- No audio spatialization; the audio layer is allocation-aware but
  positional research is queued.

This list is kept strict on purpose: **the demo page never implies a
feature the frames above do not show.**

## The honesty contract

it. When the demo gains a feature, this page gains the measurement that
proves it — in that order.
