## The demo arc

The founding prompt defines the slice as one highly developed region
and a player who must be able to do all of this — the full arc, in
the order the prompt gives it:

1. Spawn as an ordinary traveler
2. Explore the environment
3. Hunt animals
4. Gather resources
5. Craft tools
6. Build a small shelter
7. Create a farm
8. Construct a simple mechanical or electrical system
9. Automate part of the farm
10. Encounter an AI settlement
11. Trade with citizens
12. Witness an evolving local economy
13. Interact with animals
14. Fight an enemy
15. Participate in a small battle
16. Give orders to AI soldiers
17. Observe diplomacy between factions
18. Discover an unusual location
19. Experiment with engineering
20. **See the world continue to simulate without them**

The arc's last beat is the founding promise made checkable — and the
demo today honestly covers the engine foundation it stands on:
physics, movement, deterministic replay. Each further beat arrives
the same way everything here does: measured in isolation, gated,
then connected.
