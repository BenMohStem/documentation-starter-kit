## The demo arc

The demo tells one story, the same story every time, because it is
simulated rather than staged:

1. A region with terrain, light, and weather of its own;
2. A settlement of bodies and simple mechanisms that work — stacked,
   pushed, linked, weighed;
3. A player who can step away and come back to a place that kept
   running.

The arc's last beat is the project's founding promise made
checkable: *the world did not pause while the player was gone.*
