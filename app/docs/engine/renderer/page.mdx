The quiet part of the world's beauty: light, depth, distance.

The renderer is written to one aesthetic doctrine — simple geometry,
excellent light — and one engineering doctrine: every rasterization
path must pass a per-pixel oracle before it is promoted. The result
is deliberately plain and deliberately trustworthy.

## Why it stays simple

The graphics doctrine is written into the design: the world should
look simple but impressive — excellent light, strong composition,
simple geometry, beautiful atmosphere. The renderer serves that
doctrine rather than fighting it: tiles and exact depth are enough
for readable silhouettes and honest light, and everything the engine
does not spend on pixels it can spend on the world thinking.
