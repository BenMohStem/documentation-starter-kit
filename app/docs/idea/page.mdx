# The Idea

<SceneHeader scene="idea" height={240} />

<span className="wwa-page-title">The idea</span>

A game where the player learns by living inside a simulated world.

Not a story you follow — a **place**. Large, calm, consistent. An
ecology that eats and grows. An economy that prices and trades. A
civilization that farms, builds, taxes, wars, and declines by its own
arithmetic.

> **The one law:** the world must keep living even when the player
> does nothing.

## What it refuses

No magic · no attention tricks · no hand-holding · no content bloat ·
no fake complexity.

<div className="wwa-quiet-note">
