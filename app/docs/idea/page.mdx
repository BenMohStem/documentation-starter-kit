# The Idea

<SceneHeader scene="idea" height={240} />

<PageHead overline="The pitch" title="The idea" />

A game where the player learns by living inside a simulated world.

Not a story you follow — a **place**. Large, calm, consistent. An
ecology that eats and grows. An economy that prices and trades. A
civilization that farms, builds, taxes, wars, and declines by its own
arithmetic.

> **The one law:** the world must keep living even when the player
> does nothing.

## The bet, stated honestly

Most games are *authored experiences*: content placed where you'll
find it, difficulty tuned to your mood, a story waiting for your
input. This project bets the opposite direction — that a world which
runs by rule, honestly and consistently, is the deeper game:

| The authored way | The WWA way |
|---|---|
| A bear that attacks when the level needs tension | A bear that behaves like a bear, every time, whether you are watching or not |
| A war triggered by a plot beat | A war that emerges from grain, roads, tribute, and fear |
| Answers placed where you'll find them | Answers that exist because the world is consistent |
| The world pauses when you leave | The world *is what it is* — you are one thing in it |

The bet is expensive. It costs an engine measured every step, a
research library that keeps its failures, and years of building the
floor before the house. That price is the subject of the rest of
this site.
