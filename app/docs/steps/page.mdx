# Steps & Eras

<SceneHeader scene="steps" height={230} />

<span className="wwa-page-title">Steps & eras</span>

Every era numbered. Nothing renumbered. Failures kept.

The version numbers are not marketing — they are addresses. Each
number points at a folder in the archive: its design, its runs, its
report, and its verdict. When a later iteration invalidates an
earlier claim, the earlier claim is not edited — it is marked, and
the correction carries its own number.
