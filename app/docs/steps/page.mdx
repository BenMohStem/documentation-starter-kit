# Steps & Eras

<div className="wwa-atmosphere-frame"><Atmosphere variant="mist" height={150} /></div>

<span className="wwa-page-title">Steps & eras</span>

The project keeps a real log: every era is numbered, nothing is
renumbered, and failures stay in the record. This page is the honest
summary of what each era actually did — the full measurements live in
the [Research library](/research).

<div className="wwa-archive-era">

<span className="wwa-archive-era-id">v0.x – 1.0</span>
<div className="wwa-archive-era-name">Origins</div>
<div className="wwa-archive-era-note">The toolchain doctrine set: plain C, own standard layer, own build, per-iteration logging. First graphics smoke test — a triangle, then a clear screen, then a window that stayed open. Humble on purpose.</div>

</div>

<div className="wwa-archive-era">

<span className="wwa-archive-era-id">1.4 – 1.9</span>
<div className="wwa-archive-era-name">The engine lab</div>
<div className="wwa-archive-era-note">The founding measurements: how layouts of data change simulation speed (some "smarter" ones lost badly — kept as the project's first great lesson), how batching wins decay with size, what SIMD honestly buys. The habit of benchmarking everything was born here.</div>

</div>

<div className="wwa-archive-era">

<span className="wwa-archive-era-id">2.0 – 2.3</span>
<div className="wwa-archive-era-name">Broadphase & traversal</div>
<div className="wwa-archive-era-note">How to find what is near what — grids versus sorting versus brute force, measured across workload shapes. The famous result: no single method wins everywhere; the terrain was mapped honestly.</div>

</div>

<div className="wwa-archive-era">

<span className="wwa-archive-era-id">2.4 – 2.9</span>
<div className="wwa-archive-era-name">The playable demo</div>
<div className="wwa-archive-era-note">Physics became playable: bodies you can spawn, a first-person view, real input, generated sound. The demo became the permanent entry point of the project — every release must keep it working.</div>

</div>

<div className="wwa-archive-era">

<span className="wwa-archive-era-id">2.10 – 2.14</span>
<div className="wwa-archive-era-name">The mechanism lab</div>
<div className="wwa-archive-era-note">First world mechanisms measured in isolation: agents with energy budgets, regrowing resources, spontaneous division of labor from one threshold rule, environmental signal fields. The world's first primitives — proven small before being promised large.</div>

</div>

<div className="wwa-archive-era">

<span className="wwa-archive-era-id">2.15 – 2.25</span>
<div className="wwa-archive-era-name">Tiles & honesty</div>
<div className="wwa-archive-era-note">The renderer grew tiles and an exact correctness oracle; a rendering bug that had quietly collapsed geometry was caught and kept. The pattern of this project set: gates catch what eyes miss, and the record keeps what the gates catch.</div>

</div>

<div className="wwa-archive-era">

<span className="wwa-archive-era-id">2.26 – 2.52</span>
<div className="wwa-archive-era-name">The floor</div>
<div className="wwa-archive-era-note">The from-scratch standard layer: memory, strings, math, I/O, threads — hundreds of thousands of automated checks catching subtle number-parsing and formatting bugs most projects never see. The engine stopped inheriting and started owning.</div>

</div>

<div className="wwa-archive-era">

<span className="wwa-archive-era-id">2.53 – 2.98</span>
<div className="wwa-archive-era-name">Numbers & the integrated world</div>
<div className="wwa-archive-era-note">Compact number formats measured honestly (great for storage, not for compute — kept as research); the renderer's tiles promoted against their oracle; one shared spatial sense serving everything — the largest integrated win yet; the builder made fast; first adaptive-storage studies. The engine learned to study itself: incidents where the measurement was wrong were caught, retracted, and made into stronger rules.</div>

</div>

<div className="wwa-archive-era">

<span className="wwa-archive-era-id">3.x</span>
<div className="wwa-archive-era-name">The deep research line</div>
<div className="wwa-archive-era-note">The physics studied as a research subject: how warm-started contacts replay (very fast, exactly); how where data sits in memory changes how fast the world thinks; how solver convergence — not just speed — dominates what a frame costs; how a layout policy can earn the right to reorder the world, scope its claims, and decline to act when acting would lose. Every promotion gated: oracles, corrupted controls, sanitizers, repetition, two independent runs. The retracted results stayed visible; they are how the rules got strong.</div>

</div>

<div className="wwa-archive-era-note">The world's first living systems enter the actual demo: ecology, resources, animal behavior — connected, at last, into one place that runs while you watch and while you don't.</div>

</div>
