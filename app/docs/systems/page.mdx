# Systems

<div className="wwa-atmosphere-frame"><Atmosphere variant="mist" height={150} /></div>

<span className="wwa-page-title">Systems</span>

The world is designed as a small set of systems that genuinely
interact — resources feed economies, economies feed politics, politics
feeds war, war feeds hunger. Each page documents the design in depth:
what the system does, what rules produce it, what it refuses to fake,
and what has been measured so far.

<div className="wwa-docs-section">

### Living ecology

Animals that are organisms — hunger, fear, territory, memory — not
wandering decorations. Plants grow, are grazed, recover or collapse.
Predators hunt; prey flees; populations breathe. **The rule:** the
world is not balanced around the player's convenience; a bear can
outrun you. Distant regions run statistically, nearby ones
individually — detail comes with proximity, honestly. *Measured in
isolation:* energy budgets, regrowth, signal fields, spontaneous
division of labor. *Not yet in the demo.*

</div>

<div className="wwa-docs-section">

### Civilization & economy

Settlements with needs, work, and trade. Prices that mean something:
a war raises grain, a broken road starves a market, a new tool
changes everything. Civilizations have cultures, laws, and their own
arithmetic of growth and decline — brutal ones reap rebellion, kind
ones reap trust. **The rule:** no scripted outcomes; consequence is
computed, not authored. *Foundation measured:* task specialization
from one threshold rule. *Not yet in the demo.*

</div>

<div className="wwa-docs-section">

### Building & engineering

Simple, combinable mechanisms — levers, gears, shafts, wires, gates,
timers, motors — from which players build doors, farms, calculators,
and one day robots and primitive computers. **The rule:** simple
components, honest combination; no magical "computer block" —
understanding is built from what a machine visibly does. Recipes are
transformations (ore → smelting → metal), never riddles.

</div>

<div className="wwa-docs-section">

### War & battles

Armies that need food, equipment, horses, and roads — that walk, not
teleport. Battles joined personally: you ride, you command, you
retreat, you negotiate — one participant in a real melee, never an
omniscient camera. **The rule:** damage is physical — weapon, angle,
armor, anatomy — and skill plus preparation matter. War emerges from
civilization simulation, it is never spawned for spectacle.

</div>

<div className="wwa-docs-section">

### Science & discovery

Knowledge is found by experimenting — materials, mechanics,
electricity, biology — never unlocked from a menu. The player notices,
hypothesizes, tests, and either learns or is surprised. **The rule:**
the game never explains what the world can teach by being consistent.

</div>

Each system enters the playable world the same way: designed here,
measured in isolation, gated, then connected. When one ships, its page
grows a "shipped" section with what it proved.

The world-side pages — [premise](/world/premise),
[emergence](/world/emergence), [observation](/world/observation),
[roadmap](/world/roadmap) — hold the shorter, quieter versions of
these ideas. This is the deep shelf.
