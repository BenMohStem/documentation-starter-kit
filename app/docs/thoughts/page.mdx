<span className="wwa-page-title">Thoughts</span>

The project's settled positions — the design principles and hard-won
judgments that everything else references. Each thought is numbered
in the order it was settled, tagged by theme, and cites where it
came from: the founding prompt, a measured iteration, or a kept
failure.

Nothing here is aspiration that outruns evidence. Where a thought
rests on a measurement, the measurement is in the
<Link href="/research"> library</Link>; where it rests on the design,
the rule is in <Link href="/docs/design"> the constitution</Link>.

Tap a thought to read it. Filter by theme to follow one thread.
