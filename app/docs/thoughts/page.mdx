# Thoughts

<SceneHeader scene="thoughts" height={230} />

<PageHead overline="The organizing map" title="Thoughts" />

The project's organizing map — the settled positions, measured
verdicts, and design laws that everything else references. Each
thought is numbered in the order it was settled, tagged by theme,
cites where it came from (the founding prompt, a measured era, or a
kept failure), and links to where its subject lives in the
documentation.

Nothing here is aspiration that outruns evidence. Where a thought
rests on a measurement, the measurement is in the
<Link href="/research"> library</Link>; where it rests on the design,
the rule is in <Link href="/docs/design"> the constitution</Link>.

Filter by tag to follow one thread. Tap a thought to read it.
