# Timeline

<SceneHeader scene="steps" height={230} />

<PageHead overline="Chronology" title="Timeline" />

The project's chronology, kept honest. Every promotion, retraction,
incident, and milestone on this spine exists in the
<Link href="/research/ledger"> ledger</Link> with its full numbers;
every rule a scar taught is in the
<Link href="/research/doctrine"> doctrine</Link>. The spine is
deliberately sparse — only events that changed the project's
direction or its rules.

The dot colors carry meaning: **founding** and **milestones** in
moonlight, **promotions** in green (they are the fewest), and the
honest marks — **retractions**, **incidents**, **rejections** — in
red and stone, kept as deliberately as the wins.
