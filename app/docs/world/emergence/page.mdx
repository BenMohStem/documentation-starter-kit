## What emergence means here, precisely

It does not mean "surprises." It means the surprises are *not
authored*. Concretely:

- The crow calling its friends is a rule about crows — flocking,
  memory, alarm — not a scripted event with a crow skin;
- The price of bread after a war is an economy with a grain supply,
  not a designer turning a difficulty dial;
- The valley that empties when you over-hunt is a predator-prey
  system with you in it, not a scripted consequence cutscene.

The test is always the same: trace the event backward through the
rules. If the trace ends at an author, it is fake. If the trace ends
at arithmetic — hunger, supply, fear, cost — it is real. This site
only promises the second kind.

## The price of the bet

Emergence cannot be patched in later. It constrains everything
upward: the physics must be honest (a leaning stack of logs is a
lever), the ecology must balance (predators without prey starve),
the economy must close (wealth comes from somewhere). That is why
the engine and its measurement program come first — the bet is only
as good as the floor it stands on.
