## The loop the world is built around

The design's core learning loop is the game itself:

**Act → the world answers → observe what changed → form a guess →
test it → find out.**

Nothing in that loop requires a tutorial. The wolf that circles
instead of charging teaches you it is injured. The grain that costs
double in one town teaches you its road is broken. The field that
yields less each season teaches you what the soil is doing. The
player who watches accumulates something the player who rushes
cannot buy: correct expectations.

And because the simulation is deterministic, knowledge is *stable* —
what you learn about wolves stays true about wolves. The world
never re-rolls its own nature to entertain you.
