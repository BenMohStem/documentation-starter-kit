<span className="wwa-page-title">Observation</span>

No journal explains what the animal wants.

No minimap icon over the herb you need. The world keeps its own state
and shows it — in tracks, in weather, in behavior, in price. The
player who watches carefully knows things the player who rushes
cannot.

> Most games translate the world for you.
> This one asks you to learn its language — the way you learn a real
> place: pay attention, form a guess, test it, be surprised.

<div className="wwa-quiet-note">

Knowledge here is never given. It is earned — and what is earned,
stays.

</div>
