## The geography is the game's skeleton

The world is large and continuous — mountains, valleys, plains,
forests, deserts, rivers, lakes, oceans, islands, coastlines, snow
country, fertile regions, dry regions. It is divided by altitude
and geography, never by loading screens or portals:

- **Down is real.** Underground means physically descending: caves,
  underground rivers, minerals, geological formations, dangerous
  environments, ancient ruins, extremely deep regions;
- **Up is real.** The sky and the Moon are actual places at actual
  distances. Reaching them is an engineering problem, not a menu
  option — the endgame of the machine-building ladder;
- **Weather has a schedule.** Seasons, rain, drought, snow — they
  arrive by the world's own arithmetic, and everything that lives
  in it answers to them.
