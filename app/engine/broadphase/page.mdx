## No universal winner (measured, repeatedly)

- **2.0**: brute force vs uniform grid vs SAP across
  uniform/clustered/coherent workloads — grid won sparse/coherent,
  brute force won clustered, full-rebuild SAP never. No method promoted;
- **2.25**: BVH refit vs rebuild ranged 0.68×–2.75× by workload —
  "refit is not a universal replacement";
- **2.98**: linked vs packed vs packed-Morton broadphase, all exact
  against a brute-force oracle: linked wins uniform/coherent, Morton
  wins dense clusters when construction is counted. The research-only
  selector rule: `max_cell ≥ 24 → consider Morton`;
- **2.78**: an adaptive spatial manager was **REJECTED** outright — a
  state-integrity failure (storage order coupled to identity; candidate
  sets diverged). The failure is kept because it taught the design
  constraint the successful selector later obeyed.

## The layout manager arc (3.185–3.191)

The deepest locality study: whether a dynamic layout manager can decide,
every frame, *repair locally, rebuild globally, or leave alone*. The arc
is the project's doctrine in miniature — an incident caught, an
instrument rebuilt, an honest promotion:

| Iteration | What happened |
|---|---|
| 3.185 | First full test; a 1.44× promotion **retracted the same session** — fresh-process noise (59–302 cycles/contact) exceeded the effect |
| 3.186 | Instrument rebuilt: interleaved per-frame measurement, min-of-K, explicit noise floors. Confirmed: no policy beat identity on that workload — the natural order was already optimal by construction |
| 3.187 | Adversarial topology families built; **first honest promotion**: O(1) cheap-swap repair + BFS layout on hub topologies, low churn — beyond 2× noise on two independent runs |
| 3.188 | The adaptive selector: probes the live contact array; starts as identity, must earn its layout; declines to act (never-lose) on all hostile families |
| 3.189 | Chain promotion at 128K bodies; hub re-verified at 256K: **selector 50.27 vs identity 156.37 cycles/contact (3.11×)**, beating every always-policy |
| 3.190 | Order-invariant degree probe; the 1% miss resolved as instrument-bound (chain scope extended to 0–1% churn, clearing the bar ~9×); the maintained instrument's honest price recorded (+10–15 cycles never-lose) |
| 3.191 | Pre-registered, not yet run: compaction, maintenance shedding, exit hysteresis, decorrelated touch order |
