## Owned end to end

The engine links against almost nothing: its own standard surface, its
own builder, its own math, its own timing. When nothing is inherited,
nothing is mysterious — a bug in allocation is your bug, in your file,
with your comment above it. The modules:

- **core** — foundation types, deterministic RNG, telemetry;
- **physics** — Verlet integration, AABB contacts, warm-started
  manifolds, the solver research line;
- **broadphase** — uniform grids, packed cells, SAP experiments, the
  shared spatial layer;
- **renderer** — CPU tiled rasterizer (8×8 production baseline), Vulkan
  target;
- **audio** — generated PCM, allocation-aware mixing;
- **io / jobs / platform** — streaming, work grains, Win32/X11;
- **simulation / mod / perf** — the world layer, the mod ABI, the timing
  abstraction every benchmark routes through.

## What the engine is not

It is not a library showcase. Every subsystem exists because the world
needs it to keep its promises: consequence that is real requires ten
thousand bodies with mass, friction, stacking and wear — not decorative.
The engine is the price of the world, paid in C, measured every step.
