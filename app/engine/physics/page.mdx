# Physics

<span className="wwa-page-title">Physics</span>

The physics is the heart of the engine, and it is treated as a research
subject, not a checkbox.

## What runs today

Bodies with mass and friction, integrated on a fixed timestep,
colliding through exact overlap tests, resolved by impulses — the
classical core, done carefully. The solver *remembers* between frames:
contacts that persist warm-start from their last solution, which
measured as a large replay speedup at zero numerical drift on this
project's own audit.

## What is being researched

The deep question: the cost of a simulation is memory traffic, and
memory traffic depends on *where things sit*. So the project studies
layout — how arranging bodies in memory by their contact structure can
let the solver think faster — and convergence — how the number of
solver passes, and not just their speed, dominates what a frame costs.
Both lines have produced real, scoped results and honest dead ends;
the dead ends are kept, because they mapped the terrain.

## The discipline that makes it trustworthy

Every solver claim survives an exact independent oracle, a deliberately
corrupted control run (to prove the oracle can fail), sanitizer runs,
and repetition across fresh processes on a pinned core. One promoted
result was retracted within a day when repetition revealed it was
noise — that story, and the stronger rules born from it, is part of the
record. Nothing about the physics is claimed on vibes.
