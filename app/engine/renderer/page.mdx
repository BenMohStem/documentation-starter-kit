## What ships

A software tile-based rasterizer: triangles binned to 8×8-pixel screen
tiles, Pineda edge-function coverage, depth tested per pixel, RGBA8888
framebuffer — the production baseline since 2.67, when it was promoted
against an **independent direct-per-pixel oracle** with 0 mismatches
across sparse/clustered/large-overlap/coherent scenes at 2,048 and 4,096
triangles. The first-person 3D demo runs this path.

## What was kept out (with reasons)

- **In-tile / interior SIMD raster** (2.68–2.69): exactness passed, the
  speed gate failed — production unchanged;
- **Fixed-point quantized rasterization** (2.67): measured, not promoted;
- **AVX2 FP8 gather decode** (2.55): a negative result — gather decode
  measured *slower* than AVX2 BF16; kept as research;
- **Hierarchical screen-space bins** (2.23): research line, gated by the
  same direct oracle before anything ships.

## The discipline

New raster optimizations require an exact direct-reference oracle — a
candidate that changes pixels or depth semantics is dead regardless of
speed. Memory-traffic measurement comes **before** lighting work; visual
polish is deliberately postponed until the simulation earns it.
