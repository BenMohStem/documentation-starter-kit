## What the demo includes

- First-person movement through a real 3D scene (tiled renderer path);
- The physics core stepping live: bodies with mass, contacts, friction;
- Warm-started persistent manifolds — the solver remembers between frames
  (measured 70.98x replay speedup at zero drift in the micro-benchmark);
- Engine instrumentation underneath: deterministic replay, telemetry.

## What it does not include (yet)

- No animals, plants, ecology — mechanisms are prototyped and measured
  ([Emergence](/game/emergence)) but not in the shipped demo;
- No building/crafting — planned, not built;
- No save meta-world — planned.

We keep this list strict on purpose: **the demo section never implies
completion.**
