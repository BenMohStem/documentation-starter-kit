# Observation & Curiosity

Status: design philosophy, in force.

The world rewards curiosity rather than manipulation. There is no minimap
pinger, no XP bar, no achievement toast. What remains is **noticing**:

- an animal behaving strangely at dusk;
- water pooling where it never did before;
- a light that appears at the same hour each night.

## Noticing is the mechanic

In most games, the HUD is the world's narrator. In WWA the world is the
world's narrator — silently. A player who watches learns where deer drink,
which slopes hold snow, when predators hunt. That knowledge changes how you
play: where you build, when you travel, what you fear.

No red crowns above quest-givers. No glowing trails. The reward for looking
closer is more world.

## The quiet contract

The game never:

- fakes a process for effect (if water pools, something dammed it);
- schedules a "discovery moment" on a timer;
- quizzes the player or grades their understanding;
- explains a mystery the world itself can demonstrate.

Mystery without deception. Complexity without opacity. Those constraints
govern the [engineering](/engineering) as much as the design.
