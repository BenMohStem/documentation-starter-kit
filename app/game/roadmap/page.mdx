# Roadmap

Every milestone below carries its real status. Nothing is dated — dates we
cannot keep are not promises we make.

## Now — the engine core

<div className="wwa-milestone">

**M1 — Dynamic layout manager** <StatusBadge status="PROMOTED" /> (scoped)

The topology-adaptive layout research (3.185–3.187) landed its first
full-gate promotion: on community-structured contact topologies at low
churn, BFS-family layouts + an O(1) cheap-swap repair beat never-reordering
beyond the measured noise floor. The engine's solver sweep can now be managed
conditioned on measured topology — the "no universal best" doctrine,
implemented. See [Research](/research).

</div>

<div className="wwa-milestone">

**M2 — Integrated physics pipeline**

Assemble the validated pieces into one end-to-end frame: broadphase →
contact discovery → persistent manifolds → geometry epochs → warm impulse
validity → coloring → compact packets → 6-DoF solve → exact reference →
SIMD → writeback → telemetry. The 3.187 harness is the testbed.

</div>

<div className="wwa-milestone">

**M3 — Engine foundations consolidation**

Renderer (tiling, validated paths only), audio (allocation-free mixing,
voice budget), IO/streaming, jobs/memory — all mounted on the shared
locality layer. Gate: integrated engine step at frame budget with
deterministic replay.

</div>

## Next — the living world

<div className="wwa-milestone">

**M4 — Playable vertical slice**

The full pipeline inside the wandering demo: move, observe, interact
physically. All systems cooperating in one world — the mature form of the
[playable demo](/game/demo).

</div>

<div className="wwa-milestone">

**M5 — Ecosystem**

Realistic animal AI on the shared spatial layer: perception, needs, flee and
hunt, death and birth. The mechanism prototypes (metabolism, regrowth,
stigmergy, homeostasis) graduate from laboratories into the world. Gate: a
32K-entity-class integrated workload within frame budget.

</div>

## Far — the civilization

<div className="wwa-milestone">

**M6 — Civilization layers**

Economy, farming, crafting, building, engineering mechanisms, societies,
war — each as a falsifiable mechanism experiment first, promoted by
measurement only. The far vision (moon and heights, deep underworld) stays
vision-tier until the engine earns it.

</div>

## Standing rules

- Never promote on a single multiplier — complete-path cost always charged;
- Failed experiments are knowledge — preserved and visible in the
  [Journal](/journal);
- The world must run, breathe, and be worth investigating — that is the
  final validator.
