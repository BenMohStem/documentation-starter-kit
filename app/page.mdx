## What this is

A quiet, large simulation, built from scratch and measured every step.
One day: a world that keeps living whether you watch or not — rivers
carving, animals hunting, economies shifting, civilizations rising and
declining by their own arithmetic. Today: the engine that must exist
first, and the discipline that keeps it honest.

</Reveal>

## Where it stands

A playable demonstration of the engine core runs today — physics,
first-person movement, deterministic replay. The world's living
systems are being measured in isolation before they enter it. Nothing
ships that has not survived its gates; failures are kept and learned
from.

</Reveal>
