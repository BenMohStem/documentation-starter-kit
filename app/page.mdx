# World Without Answers

**A game about a world that does not explain itself.**

You wake in a valley with no tutorial, no quest markers, and no explanations.
Rivers carve, trees grow, animals hunt and flee — because of rules, not
scripts. What you understand of this world, you earn by watching it.

## Where the project stands

| Layer | Status |
|---|---|
| Native C engine core (physics, broadphase, renderer, audio, IO, jobs) | **Runs today** — `demo-check` gated |
| Playable 3D demonstration | **Runs today** — see [Demo](/game/demo) |

| Civilization: economy, farming, building, war | **Planned** — years of work, honestly labeled |

Nothing on this site claims more than has been measured. Failed experiments
stay visible in the [Journal](/journal).

## Why an engine, not a mod

The goal is a world where **consequence is real**: ten thousand bodies with
mass, friction, stacking and wear — not decorative. That requires owning the
whole stack, in C, measured every step. The
[Engineering](/engineering) section shows what that costs and what it buys.
