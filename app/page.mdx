**A game about a world that does not explain itself — built on an engine
that refuses to lie about its own numbers.**

You wake as an ordinary wanderer. No tutorial, no quest markers, no chosen
one. The world runs — rivers carve, animals hunt and flee, economies shift —
whether you act or not. What you understand of it, you earn by watching it.
Underneath is a from-scratch C engine, measured at every step: every claim
this project makes is bound to the measurement that produced it, and the
failures are kept.

## How this project is different

Two laws govern everything. The first is engineering:

<div className="wwa-law">

*Organize the data so that the cheaper physics calculation is also the
cheaper memory traversal.*

</div>

The second is epistemic: **the world's complexity must be earned by
emergence, never faked by scripts** — simple rules, faithfully applied,
every frame. Everything else follows: no magic, no portals, no HUD
hand-holding, and a research pipeline where a paper can propose but never
decide. A promotion in this project survives an oracle, a negative
control, sanitizers, fresh-process repetition, and two independent runs —
or it does not ship.
