# The Doctrine

## The gates (D1–D8)

1. **D1 — the promotion bar.** A beats B only when the median beats, the
   effect exceeds **2× the measured noise floor**, on **both of two
   independent runs** (≥5 repetitions each), the oracle passes every
   row, and the negative control fails as armed. No gate, no promotion.
2. **D2 — the never-lose bar.** A promoted policy must not lose to
   identity anywhere within its claimed scope: |median diff| ≤ 2× max
   noise floors, plus ≥99% correct recommended actions.
3. **D4 — instrument before policy.** The measuring instrument is
   validated *before it times anything* — probe audits as confusion
   matrices, agreement ≥99% per family. The instrument that failed its
   audit never gated a claim.
4. **D6 — a control that cannot fail is not a control.** Every benchmark
   carries an armed negative control (`--corrupt-endpoint`) that must
   flip the oracle.
5. **D7 — operator freeze.** The physics operators (`step_one`, `k_one`,
   `vn_one`) and the FP tree are byte-frozen across research iterations;
   changes are additive, verified by mechanical diff.
6. **D8 — no post-hoc threshold tuning.** Frozen thresholds stay frozen.
   Failures are findings, not knobs; new values are pre-registered for
   *future* iterations.

Plus the 3.186 measurement law: **interleaved** policy execution per
frame (shared thermal/scheduler conditions), **min-of-K** sampling with
explicit noise-floor columns, ≥5 fresh processes per claim.

## The pre-registration rule

Every iteration's DESIGN.md is written **before any run**. Deviations
get dated, append-only amendments — never silent rewrites. Predictions
are registered, including the predictions about the instrument itself;
when the 3.189 probe audit refuted the pre-registered chain-probe
predictions, the refutation was recorded before any policy timing
existed.

## The cost law

<div className="wwa-law">

*T_amortized(H) = (T_prepare + T_remap)/H + T_solve(H)*

</div>

An organization must repay itself over horizon H. Since 3.178, every
promotion charges organization + remap + maintenance against savings.
The corollary that became a measured fact: **no layout is universally
best** — the selector's job is to know when *not* to act.

## The instrument price law (3.190)

A maintained instrument has a visible price, and the doctrine predicts
its shape: an O(nc)-per-rebuild instrument cannot follow an
O(epoch)-churn schedule at 32K scale. The mix battery flip (3.190's
maintained-degree selector losing what 3.189's stateless selector won)
was caught, priced, and published — and the fifth gate joined the law
set: **every published cost number must survive a back-of-envelope cost
model**, because content gates cannot see a 64× overallocation.

## The hierarchy of evidence

From the benchmark protocol, in ascending order: math sanity →
independent reference → differential checks → adversarial randomized →
sanitizers → source-to-report consistency → fresh-process timing → CPU
affinity/migration detection → randomized order → compiler/ISA
sensitivity → p50+MAD+raw samples → physical-quality normalization →
**complete-frame accounting**. No single timing number is sufficient for
promotion — time is one coordinate of a tuple that includes penetration,
residuals, torque error, energy drift, warm-state retention, bytes per
constraint, and SIMD lane utilization.

## What this buys

A number on this site means: measured on a named machine, gated by this
doctrine, reproducible from the record. That is the whole difference
between a claim and a hope — and it is why the failures section is the
most valuable part of this project.
