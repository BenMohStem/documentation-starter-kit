# Era Timeline

The project's whole arc, era by era, in its own numbering. Iterations
are never renumbered; later entries never erase earlier ones.

<div className="wwa-era">

<span className="wwa-era-range">v0.x – 1.0</span> — **Origins.** The
toolchain doctrine: gcc MSYS2 UCRT, no CMake, own std layers, per-
iteration logging. First Vulkan smoke lab: instance → surface →
swapchain → clear → present. VS2019 demoted to nvcc host only.

</div>

<div className="wwa-era">

<span className="wwa-era-range">1.4 – 1.9</span> — **The engine lab.**
The founding benchmarks: indexed LOD loses at ~2.30× full-update cost;
renderer batch wins measured (9.37× at 64 points, decaying with size);
the DOD sweep that produced the anti-slogan result (AoS beat SoA and
AoSoA on 1M agents); AVX2 3-thread 5.15 ms vs 10.05 ms scalar.

</div>

<div className="wwa-era">

<span className="wwa-era-range">2.0 – 2.3</span> — **Broadphase and
traversal.** The 2.0 broadphase matrix (no universal winner — the
measured fact); persistence-vs-traversal locality; the 2.3 traversal
benchmark: LOD-grouped AoS 2.281× over plain AoS at 131,072 bodies.

</div>

<div className="wwa-era">

<span className="wwa-era-range">2.4 – 2.9</span> — **The playable
demo.** 2.4: playable physics (96 bodies, mouse-spawn, generated PCM
audio). 2.6: first-person 3D slice with CPU rasterizer. 2.7: the
real-camera-input bug found. 2.8: uniform-grid broadphase merged into
the demo. 2.9: the player becomes a real physics body.

</div>

<div className="wwa-era">

<span className="wwa-era-range">2.10 – 2.14</span> — **The mechanism
lab.** 2.13: the shared spatial layer (1.75× at 32 agents → 6.65× at
1024) + Sugarscape ecology mode. 2.14: response-threshold tasks —
spontaneous specialization from one rule, measured over 21.9M ticks.

</div>

<div className="wwa-era">

<span className="wwa-era-range">2.15 – 2.25</span> — **Signal, tiles,
and honesty.** Stigmergy fields (2.15); homeostatic drives (2.20); the
renderer correctness defect where cube faces collapsed to one triangle
(2.21) and the tile-size matrix that followed; hierarchical screen bins
(2.23) beside the Windows `0xC0000005` incident — kept verbatim; BVH
refit measured 0.68×–2.75× (2.25).

</div>

<div className="wwa-era">

<span className="wwa-era-range">2.27 – 2.52</span> — **Packaging and the
std layer.** Windows desktop package with crash filter (2.27); the
native builder GUI (2.32); the canonical WWA std era — 20 headers, the
own-the-floor doctrine (2.37–2.52).

</div>

<div className="wwa-era">

<span className="wwa-era-range">2.53 – 2.69</span> — **Quantization and
the oracle'd renderer.** The full FP16→NVFP4 family (2.53–2.54);
consumer-kernel quantization with the AVX2 FP8 gather negative (2.55);
fused BF16 setup through the real raster (2.63); 8×8 tiles promoted
against the independent direct-per-pixel oracle (2.67); SIMD interior
paths exact-but-slow (2.68–2.69).

</div>

<div className="wwa-era">

<span className="wwa-era-range">2.70 – 2.98</span> — **Windows boundary
and the integrated world.** The 2.70 header-boundary doctrine; all
benchmark timers routed through the owned perf abstraction (2.73);
grouped-contiguous LOD re-measured at 0.41× full cost (2.74); the
rejected adaptive spatial manager (2.78); the shared spatial layer
promoted (2.79); dirty-set migration data contract (2.83); the mod ABI
with batch-plane economics (2.84–2.86); the builder graph-locality fix
(2.95); the Morton broadphase study and adaptive-selector rule (2.98).

</div>

<div className="wwa-era">

<span className="wwa-era-range">3.50 – 3.172</span> — **The physics
research chain.** Warm-start replay 70.98× at zero drift; the Delassus
oracle at 2.22e-16; the Coulomb cone violation finding (95% of
independent-clamp trials); K-cache epochs investigated and honestly
closed negative; the arithmetic-tree contract; one-packet numerical
audits; the shared packed world (5.17–5.77× across churn rates).

</div>

<div className="wwa-era">

<span className="wwa-era-range">3.173 – 3.184</span> — **Layout
research.** Cache/layout policies measured: 224.4 vs 45.6 cycles/contact
spread; the T_amortized cost model born; the mean-reuse-distance
predictor failed; honesty rules hardened.

</div>

<div className="wwa-era">

<span className="wwa-era-range">3.185 – 3.191</span> — **The manager
earns its keep.** The incident (3.185), the rebuilt instrument (3.186),
adversarial families and the first scoped promotion (3.187), the
adaptive selector (3.188), chains and 256K verification (3.189), the
order-invariant degree probe with its honest price (3.190), and the
pre-registered next step (3.191).

</div>

<div className="wwa-era">

<span className="wwa-era-range">3.205 – 3.213</span> — **The solver
line, corrected in public.** Contact-order dependency (1.27–1.88×); the
3.206 retraction; Fischer–Burmeister residuals; time-to-target as the
metric; SGS rejected; PSOR promising and workload-dependent; adaptive
PSOR pre-committed as the next experiment.

</div>

The next era is unwritten. The standing orders hold: preserve all
negative results; the demo is the permanent entry point; research
creates hypotheses, and WWA benchmarks decide.
