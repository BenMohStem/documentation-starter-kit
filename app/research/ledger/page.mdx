<span className="wwa-page-title">The evidence ledger</span>

Every claim the record has ruled on, with its verdict. This is the
evidence spine the [library](/research) shelves organize around:
each entry names the claim as it was made, what the measurement
showed, why the verdict fell where it did, and — where the verdict
is a promotion — the exact scope the promotion carries.

Filter by status to see the shape of the record: the promotions are
the fewest shelves, and they earned it.
