# The Library

<SceneHeader scene="research" height={230} />

<span className="wwa-page-title">Research library</span>

The project keeps its own archive, in the spirit of a research
library: every experiment, every measurement, every failure —
numbered, dated, and organized by era. Nothing is renumbered; nothing
is cleaned up for presentation; retracted results stay, marked, so
the same mistake is never made twice.

The standards here are the project's own, and they are strict:

- **Hypotheses are not results.** An idea — from a paper, a hunch, or
  an industry practice — is a hypothesis until the reference machine
  has measured it on the actual workloads;
- **The instrument is audited before it measures.** A measurement
  tool that has not proven itself against a known failure does not
  gate anything;
- **A claim that loses is kept.** The negatives are the library's
  most valuable shelves — they are how the terrain got mapped;
- **Numbers carry their context.** Every result names the machine,
  the workload, and the gate that produced it, or it does not
  appear.

## How the record is governed

The [doctrine](/research/doctrine) defines what a measurement must
survive before it becomes a claim. The [ledger](/research/ledger)
carries every claim with its verdict — the promotions, the
retractions, and the honest negatives, each with its scope. It grew
from real incidents — including two promotions that were retracted
the same session when repetition exposed them as noise. Those
incidents, and every one after, are preserved as part of the record.

## A note on this page

The full shelf — every iteration file with its exact numbers, run
captures, and audit trails — exists in the project's archive. It is
being transferred to this library carefully, era by era, with each
entry checked against its source before publication. The shelves
fill as they are verified: unhurried, on purpose, like everything
else here.
