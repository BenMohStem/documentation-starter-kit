## Audio

- **Allocation-free mixing**: the mixer never allocates — voices, busses,
  and buffers are reserved up front;
- **Voice budget**: a fixed voice count with priority-based culling; the
  budget is a design contract, not a tuning knob discovered in production;
- **Consumer-mask integration**: audio asks the shared spatial layer which
  emitters matter for the listener this frame — the same structure physics
  and AI read.

## IO & streaming

- Streaming with an explicit budget: what loads, when, and what evicts are
  decisions the engine measures rather than hopes;
- Deterministic ordering for replay: streaming events must not perturb the
  simulation's determinism — the replay gate applies to IO like everything
  else.

## What is next

The M3 consolidation mounts these on the shared foundation at the frame
budget. No audio or IO feature ships without its allocation and budget
evidence.
