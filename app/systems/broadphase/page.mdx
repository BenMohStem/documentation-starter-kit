# Broadphase

import StatusBadge from "@/components/status-badge";

Status: <StatusBadge status="FOUNDATION" /> — measured, workload-conditional.

The broadphase finds candidate collision pairs before the narrow phase
spends contact math on them. The engine's lesson here is one of the oldest in
the project: **no structure is universally best** — the 2.0 benchmark
measured brute force, uniform grids, and sweep-and-prune across workloads
and found the winner changes with body count, clustering, and motion.

## What runs today

- Sweep-and-prune and uniform-grid paths, selected by workload class;
- The shared spatial locality layer as the foundation both paths read from;
- Adaptive selection research (2.26) preserved as the direction for
  automatic switching.

## The honest numbers (from the project's history)

The 2.0 benchmark table — brute/grid/SAP across scattered, clustered, and
moving workloads — is preserved in the archives; its conclusion is standing
law: every broadphase claim is scoped to its measured workload, and the
website never quotes a winner without its workload.

## Next
