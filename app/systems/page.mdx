## The architecture law

All systems mount on **one shared spatial locality layer**: a single world
grid with per-consumer masks. Physics reads contacts through it; AI queries
neighborhoods through it; audio asks which emitters matter; the renderer
culls with it. No system keeps its own private copy of the world.

This is the foundation the whole engine is built on — measured, integrated,
and promoted (the ~5.7x integrated-workload win, host-measured).

## The engineering rules that bind every system

- C-first, warning-clean, sanitizer-gated, `demo-check` as the standing gate;
- allocation-free hot paths (the audio mixer never allocates; the solver
  never allocates);
- deterministic replay: same inputs, same world, same bits;
- every optimization claim carries its measurement and its status label.
