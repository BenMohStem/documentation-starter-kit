# Emergence

Engine status: <StatusBadge status="RESEARCH ONLY" /> — mechanism prototypes
exist; a living emergent world does not exist yet.

Emergence is the wager of the whole project: that a small number of
understandable rules, faithfully applied, can produce structures nobody
explicitly scripted.

## What has been measured so far

- shared spatial locality serving physics, AI, audio and rendering consumers
  from one structure (integrated workload: ~5.7x vs duplicated per-consumer
  grids on the development machine — host measurement, not a guarantee);
- mechanism prototypes (metabolism, regrowth, stigmergy signals,
  homeostatic drives) evaluated in small deterministic laboratories.

## What has not been shown yet

- an ecosystem that behaves like an ecosystem;
- societies, economies, or civilizations with believable dynamics.

We refuse to imply these exist before they do.
