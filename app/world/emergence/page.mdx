## The law

<div className="wwa-law">

*Simple rules → complex interactions → emergent behavior.*

</div>

The whole game depends on this. Twenty reusable systems that interact
beat two thousand scripted items. The engine is built so that the cheap
rule applied honestly every frame can produce what a script never can:
consequence that was never authored.

## What has been measured in isolation

The mechanism laboratory (era 2.12–2.20) prototyped the world's first
primitives as deterministic, benchmarked modes — not shipped features:

- **Metabolism & resources** — a Sugarscape-style ecology mode: agents
  with energy budgets, regrowing resource fields, measured over millions
  of ticks;
- **Response-threshold tasks** — 4,096 agents × 4 tasks × 600 steps
  produced spontaneous specialization (counts 2608 / 496 / 496 / 496)
  from one threshold rule — division of labor without a manager;
- **Stigmergy** — environmental signal fields: agents communicate by
  modifying the world, the first candidate primitive for an ecology that
  organizes itself;
- **Homeostatic drives** — need dynamics that stay stable without a
  script's attention.

Each prototype carries its own benchmark record; each is a hypothesis
about the world, kept until the engine can carry it at scale.

## What emergence will never be allowed to mean

A chaos patch is not emergence. When a mechanism is proposed, it must
define its oracle (the measurable thing that proves it works), its
negative control (what must fail if the oracle is fake), and its
workloads. The same doctrine that governs the physics solver governs the
ecology: measured, gated, promoted or recorded as knowledge — never
assumed.

## What the world wants, eventually

Animals that are organisms, not props: hunger, fear, territory, memory.
A bear can outrun you; the world is not balanced for the player's
convenience. Plants grow, are over-hunted, recover — or don't.
Civilizations farm, trade, tax, feud, and decline from their own
arithmetic. Every one of these is a research thread (see
[Research corpus](/research/corpus)) with a standing order: mechanisms
enter the engine only when they survive measurement.
