<span className="wwa-page-title">Observation</span>

In this world, noticing is the mechanic.

There is no journal that explains what an animal wants. No minimap
icon over the herb you need. The world keeps its own state and shows
it — in tracks, in weather, in behavior, in price. The player who
watches carefully knows things the player who rushes cannot.

This is a deliberate inversion of the usual contract. Most games
translate the world for you. This one asks you to learn its language:
the way you learn a real place — by paying attention, forming a guess,
testing it, and being surprised.

The same is true of the project itself. The engine is measured so its
behavior can be trusted; the world is simulated so its behavior can be
read. Understanding is never given here. It is earned — and that is
the point.
