# Observation

<StatusBadge status="FOUNDATION" /> — design philosophy, in force.

The world rewards curiosity rather than manipulation. There is no notification
queue, no engagement loop, no fear of missing out. What remains is noticing:
an animal behaving strangely, water flowing somewhere it should not, a light
that appears at the same hour each night.

The player who looks closer finds more world. That is the whole mechanic.

## Evidence over assertion

Everywhere in WWA — engine and website alike — a claim is worth exactly as
much as the evidence behind it. This site labels each experiment with its
promotion status, preserves failed experiments, and links claims to their
sources. The [Research](/research) section shows the method; the
[Journal](/journal) shows the history.
