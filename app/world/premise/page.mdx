# The World Does Not Explain Itself

<StatusBadge status="FOUNDATION" /> — the design premise.

A world can be full of consequence and still explain nothing. Rivers carve
valleys because water flows downhill; trees grow because soil holds more than
stone. None of these processes narrates itself. Understanding is something an
observer earns by watching, testing, and being wrong in public.

WWA is built on that premise: simple rules, faithfully applied, with no hidden
answers and no scripted revelations.

> A simulation, a game, and an engineering experiment about observation,
> consequence, emergence, and the search for truth.

## What exists today

- a native C engine core (physics, broadphase, renderer, audio, IO, jobs);
- a playable 3D demonstration of the current engine;
- a research program measuring its own optimizations to destruction.

## What is deliberately not here yet

- large-scale civilization simulation (planned, not built);
- ecology and society as living systems (planned, not built);
- any promise about when.

The pages under World describe the philosophy; the pages under Systems describe
what the machinery actually does today.
