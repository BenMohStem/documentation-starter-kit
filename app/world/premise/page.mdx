# The Premise

<span className="wwa-page-title">The world</span>

A large, quiet world — mountains, forests, rivers, seas, and caves that
go deep — generated, but coherent: everything in it follows from
rules, not from scripts.

You arrive in it as an ordinary traveler. No tutorial, no quest
markers, no chosen one. You can stay a wanderer forever, or become a
hunter, a farmer, a builder, an engineer, a soldier, a ruler — the
world does not push you.

The one law the design never breaks: **the world keeps living whether
you act or not.** Leave for a season and come back: the farm has grown
or failed, the prices have moved, the war you ignored has its own
history. Nothing waits for you, and nothing explains itself.

## What it is not

- **No magic.** No spells, no portals, no arbitrary physics. Everything
  that happens has a cause, and causes can be found.
- **No noise for attention.** No achievement pings, no reward loops
  engineered to pull you back. The intended feeling after leaving is
  calm, not craving.
- **No hand-holding.** The world communicates by being consistent:
  animals flee hunters, fire spreads, water flows downhill. What you
  learn, you learn by watching — and by experimenting.

## Why it is built this slowly

A world whose behavior is real has to stand on an engine whose behavior
is understood — every rule simulated faithfully, every frame. That is
the work in progress now: the engine, measured at every step, so the
world built on it never has to cheat.
