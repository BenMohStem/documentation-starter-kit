## The principle

The roadmap is not a feature wishlist; it is a research program. Each step
exists because a measured result opened it, and the next step is always
the one with the highest expected information. Nothing below is dated —
dates are not a claim this project makes.

## Now (engine foundations, in progress)

- **Adaptive broadphase selector** — choose between full rebuild and
  incremental pair maintenance using previous-frame dirty fraction, pair
  churn, and measured costs, without future-frame knowledge (2.94's
  pair-delta is the candidate: exact over 63 cases, faster at low churn,
  crossovers at high churn — pair *exits* are part of correctness);
- **Solver convergence** — the 3.207–3.213 line: projected over-relaxation
  is the strongest current lead (chain workload: 28–31 passes at ω=1.5
  vs 93–94 at ω=1.0; one case fell from 50.4M to 15.4M cycles). The
  pre-committed next experiment is **adaptive PSOR** — locally estimated
  ω with immediate fallback to ω=1, evaluated on held-out
  topology/churn streams;
- **Layout-manager engine port** — 3.191's pre-registrations: tombstone
  compaction, maintenance shedding, exit hysteresis, decorrelated harness
  touch order. The degree table's economics (amortize over world
  lifetimes, never per-rebuild) are the design axis.

## Next (the world's first systems)

- Packed FP4/FP6 fused renderer packet consumption + table-free
  vectorized FP8 decode (quantization stays storage/transport research —
  measured, not assumed);
- Renderer memory-traffic measurement *before* any lighting work;
- Audio spatialization with bounded voice budgets;
- Windows/MSYS2 + NVCC + interactive Vulkan: the standing target-machine
  acceptance gates.

## Later (the living world)

- Ecology: metabolism, regrowth, stigmergy, homeostasis promoted from
  isolated prototypes into one shared world;
- Civilization: farming, economy, diplomacy, war — each entering through
  the same oracle/negative-control/workload gates as the solver;
- The engineering ladder: mining → metallurgy → chemistry → electrical
  logic → computing → robotics → space. The Moon is reached by altitude
  and technology — never by a portal.

## Standing orders

1. Preserve all negative results — they are the project's immune system.
2. The demo is the permanent entry point; `demo-check` gates every
   release.
3. Research creates hypotheses; WWA benchmarks decide.
