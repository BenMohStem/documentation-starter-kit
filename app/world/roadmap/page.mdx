The plan follows the foundation, not the calendar: the world can only
be as honest as the engine underneath it.

## Done — the floor exists

- A from-scratch standard layer and native build system, tested to
  hundreds of thousands of automated checks;
- A physics core that runs, steps, and replays deterministically;
- A playable first-person demonstration of that core, kept working at
  every release;
- The research habit: measured experiments, honest negative results,
  and a literature pipeline that feeds ideas into the engine.

## Now — the engine grows up

- The solver and the memory system are being tuned together — where a
  body sits in memory changes how fast the world can think about it;
- The spatial foundation: one shared sense of "what is near what",
  serving physics, sound, sight, and AI alike — already measured as a
  large integrated win;
- Incremental broadphase: when things move, only what moved gets
  recomputed.

## Next — the world wakes up

- The first living systems enter the actual world: ecology, resource
  flows, animal behavior — the isolated experiments connected at last;
- Settlements: needs, work, trade — the beginning of an economy;
- Weather, water, and growth: the processes that make a place feel
  inhabited long before anyone lives there.

## Later — civilization, engineering, history

- Farming to industry; machines, electricity, and the path toward
  computing;
- War, diplomacy, and consequence at the scale of kingdoms;
- And one day, far up: the Moon — reached the only way that means
  anything, by building the machines that climb.

No dates. The rule is simply: **measure, then trust; promote, then
build.** The order of the world follows the order of proof.
