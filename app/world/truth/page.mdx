# The Search for Truth

Status: design direction.

The world is built so that a careful observer can find signs of order:
evidence, in the world's own terms, that the world is the work of an
intelligence — kindness rewarded, cruelty costing, creation pointing past
itself.

The game never states a conclusion. It never quizzes the player. It offers a
world in which seeking is worth the seeker's time, and lets what is found be
found honestly.

> Wonder without manipulation. Mystery without deception. Complexity without
> opacity.

Those constraints govern the website you are reading as much as the game.
